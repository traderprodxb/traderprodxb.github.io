Position Size

Position sizing is the core discipline of the system.

Every instrument carries a fixed dollar-distance target. Contract size is calculated so that distance equals approximately 6% of current equity. Size must always be a whole multiple of the instrument minimum.

⚠️ Hard Rule

If the planned 6% dollar amount is smaller than the instrument’s minimum distance, the trade is skipped. This prevents oversizing.

On the following conditions, position size is cut in half or trading is paused:

⚠ High ATR / elevated volatility days
📈 Major economic data releases
🏛 FOMC decisions
🌍 Geopolitical or black-swan market conditions

Note on fixed dollar distances and changing prices

The dollar-distance targets were calibrated at earlier average price levels. Because the targets remain fixed in dollars, a large change in the underlying price can cause the same dollar distance to represent a slightly different percentage move. This may result in a modestly higher or lower contract size than what would be considered perfectly optimal under current conditions. The system keeps the distances fixed for consistency and simplicity. The 6% equity target remains the guide; actual results simply move in a narrow band around it. Manual size reduction remains available whenever the effective percentage feels excessive.

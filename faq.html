Frequently Asked Questions

Systemic parameters, execution mechanics, and BlackBull infrastructure documentation.

How do the system rules protect capital?

Every trade is strictly constrained by a hard 6.0% equity Stop-Loss and a matching 6.0% equity Take-Profit, calculated precisely from live account equity at the moment of execution. Position size is algorithmically matched to instrument volatility so that price target completion equals exactly ±6.0%. Only one trade is open at any time. Non-negotiable system protocols prohibit averaging, martingale adjustments, position scaling, or discretionary overrides after order placement.

What is the exact risk/reward model?

The mathematical model operates on a strict 1:1 risk-to-reward ratio. A successful trade yields +6.0% equity growth, while an invalidation caps loss at exactly –6.0%. This baseline ratio remains static across all equity stages, trading instruments, and account sizes without exception.

How does position sizing actually work?

Position sizing integrates fixed dollar-distance targets per instrument with maximum available leverage parameters. Required unit volume is dynamically derived so that hitting the target distance produces an exact 6% equity movement. Executed lot sizes are strictly constrained to valid exchange multiples (e.g., USTech minimum size and increments are strictly 0.10 lots).

Why are trading operations restricted to eight instruments?

Execution is strictly bounded to USTech, XAUUSD, WTI Crude Oil, EURUSD, USDJPY, BTCUSD, ETHUSD, and Tesla. These assets provide superior institutional liquidity, tight spreads, predictable volatility profiles, and broker contract specs required to cleanly run the 6.0% risk distance model.

What is the full compounding path to $4,000,000?

Capital scaling progresses across four sequential 10× Major Stages: $400 → $4,000 → $40,000 → $400,000 → $4,000,000. Each Major Stage requires completing seven Mini Stages. Each Mini Stage is systematically broken into five Micro Targets. Executing five consecutive winning setups achieves one Mini Stage milestone.

What happens after a losing trade?

If an invalidation occurs, the active Micro Target is re-attempted using recalibrated position sizes based on the updated (lower) equity balance. The 6.0% risk cap is never increased to compensate for drawdown.

Is this a signal service, course, or managed-account offering?

No. TraderProDXB is purely a public accountability ledger documenting proprietary capital scaling. No trading signals are distributed, no commercial courses are offered, and third-party capital management is strictly prohibited.

How is the track record verified?

Execution performance is tracked through read-only API integration linking BlackBull Markets live trading accounts directly to Myfxbook. All trade logs, equity curves, and drawdowns are verified independently and updated automatically.

Which execution broker is utilized and why?

All trades are executed through BlackBull Markets under an institutional ECN/STP environment. Direct market access, fast fill speeds, micro-lot contract precision, and zero dealing-desk intervention are essential technical requirements for the position calculator model to run without execution slippage.

Why are Mondays and Fridays excluded from standard trading?

Mondays frequently exhibit erratic weekend gap-fill behavior, while Fridays carry structural end-of-week liquidity contraction and gap risk. Standard market operations run Tuesday through Thursday. Weekend trading is restricted to crypto setups (BTCUSD / ETHUSD) targeting early geopolitical reversals before traditional markets open.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear institutional answers regarding the TraderProDXB system and BlackBull integration.

How do the system rules protect capital?

Every trade is constrained by a hard 6% equity Stop-Loss and a matching 6% equity Take-Profit measured on account equity at the moment of entry. Position size is calculated so that the planned price distance produces exactly this 6% move. Only one trade is open at any time. There is no averaging, no martingale, no scaling in, and no discretionary override after the order is live.

What is the exact risk/reward model?

Risk and reward are identical (1:1). A winning trade targets +6% of current equity. A losing trade is limited to –6% of current equity. The system never alters these percentages regardless of account size, instrument, or stage.

How does position sizing actually work?

Position size combines a fixed dollar-distance target per instrument with the maximum leverage allowed on that instrument. The required number of units is calculated so that a price move equal to the stated distance equals ±6% of current equity. Size must be a whole multiple of the instrument’s minimum contract.

Why these eight instruments only?

They provide sufficient liquidity, relatively predictable session behaviour, and leverage levels that allow the fixed dollar-distance model to function cleanly.

What is the full compounding path to $4,000,000?

Four sequential Major Stages of 10×: $400 → $4,000 → $40,000 → $400,000 → $4,000,000. Each Major Stage contains seven Mini Stages. Each Mini Stage is divided into five Micro Targets. An ideal day completes one Mini Stage with five winning Micro Targets.

What happens after a losing trade?

The same Micro Target is re-attempted from the new (lower) equity. The 6% rule is never changed.

Is this a signal service, course, or managed-account offering?

No. This is a public accountability ledger of one trader’s live capital. No signals are distributed, no courses are sold, and no accounts are managed for others.

How is the track record verified?

The live account is linked to Myfxbook via BlackBull’s read-only investor API. Results cannot be edited or selectively reported.

Which broker is used and why?

All capital is traded through BlackBull Markets under an institutional ECN/STP environment that supports precise execution of the calculated position sizes.

Why are Mondays and Fridays excluded?

Mondays are typically too turbulent with unexpected trends. Fridays carry elevated end-of-week risk of unwanted positions. Weekends are traded on crypto for geopolitical reversal setups that appear before traditional markets open.

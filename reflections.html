Reflections & Psychological Notes

Documenting the mental state, physiological responses, and psychological endurance of mechanical day trading.

Mon Jul 27, 2026 | Win and Loss Streaks

TraderProDXB: Is it normal to feel an intense rush of relief or ease in the brain when a losing streak reverses, and physical tension or head contractions during losses?

Gemini AI: Yes, this is completely normal in day trading. When you experience a losing streak, your nervous system triggers a localized threat response, releasing cortisol and adrenaline which often manifests physically as tension, scalp tightening, or head contractions. The brain interprets consecutive losses as a survival threat to resources.

Conversely, when a losing streak breaks and a win occurs, there is an immediate surge of dopamine and a massive parasympathetic nervous system discharge—the "rush of relief" or mental decompression. Awareness of these physiological cycles is the key to maintaining machine-like discipline and preventing emotional overrides.
